Services & Programs

Access government services and programs designed to serve your needs

📋

Business License

Apply for new business licenses and renew existing ones for commercial operations.

Processing Time: 5-7 business days
Fee: $150 - $500
Requirements: Business plan, Tax ID, Proof of address
💰

Tax Registration

Register for state and local business taxes, sales tax permits.

Processing Time: 3-5 business days
Fee: $50 - $200
Requirements: Business license, EIN, Ownership documentation
🏠

Property Tax

Pay property taxes online, view assessment records, apply for exemptions.

Processing Time: Immediate
Fee: Varies by property value
Requirements: Property ID, Tax bill number
🏗️

Building Permit

Apply for residential and commercial construction permits.

Processing Time: 10-15 business days
Fee: $100 - $2,000
Requirements: Architectural plans, Site survey, Contractor license
🎓

Student Financial Aid

Apply for government scholarships, grants, and student loan programs.

Processing Time: 4-6 weeks
Fee: No application fee
Requirements: Academic records, Financial statements, Admission letter
🏥

Public Health Insurance

Apply for government-sponsored health insurance programs.

Processing Time: 2-3 weeks
Fee: Income-based premiums
Requirements: Income verification, Citizenship proof, Household size
🚗

Driver's License

Apply for new driver's license, renewals, and replacements.

Processing Time: 7-10 business days
Fee: $40 - $80
Requirements: Identity proof, Residence proof, Vision test
🚙

Vehicle Registration

Register new vehicles, renew registrations, transfer ownership.

Processing Time: 5-7 business days
Fee: $50 - $150
Requirements: Title, Insurance, Safety inspection
