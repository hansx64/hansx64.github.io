About Brew Ha

More than just a cafe, Brew Ha is the neighborhood's home base for craft, comfort, and conversation.
We created this space to be your ideal "third place"—the perfect spot between
home and work where you can recharge, connect, and enjoy a moment of honest-to-goodness perfection.

Our mission is simple: Elevate the everyday.

We are obsessive about the details. Our baristas, led by Head Barista Liam Chen, treat coffee
as both a science and an art, meticulously weighing, grinding, and pulling every shot to ensure an
unparalleled experience in every cup. This dedication extends to our kitchen, where Lead Baker
Sofia Rodriguez crafts fresh, flaky, and unforgettable pastries daily, often before the sun comes up.

At Brew Ha, we believe that quality should never be stuffy. Whether you’re grabbing a speedy
morning espresso, settling in for an afternoon working session, or sharing a laugh with friends,
you'll always find a warm welcome. Thanks to our Manager Eli Vance, community is at our core—
we're here to serve and support the people who make this neighborhood special.

Stop in, slow down, and taste the passion that goes into everything we do.
