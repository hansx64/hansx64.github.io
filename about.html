Our Mission

Our Vision

A connected community where government services are easily accessible, public information is readily available, and citizen engagement drives continuous improvement.

Our History

2005

Digital Transformation

Launched first online services portal, allowing citizens to access services remotely.

2015

Mobile Initiative

Introduced mobile-responsive design and dedicated government mobile applications.

2024

Current Platform

Launched this comprehensive information portal with enhanced accessibility features.

Legislative Mandate

Our department operates under the Public Information Access Act of 1994 and the Digital Government Services Act of 2010, which mandate:

  • Provision of timely and accurate government information
  • Ensuring equal access to services for all citizens
  • Maintaining transparency in government operations
  • Protecting citizen privacy and data security
  • Promoting digital literacy and accessibility
