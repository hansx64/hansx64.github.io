Frequently Asked Questions

Public documents can be accessed directly through this portal. For documents not available online, submit a formal request through our Public Records Request form available in the Forms section.

Most documents are available in PDF format. Some datasets may be available in CSV or Excel formats through our Open Data portal.

Most public documents are free to access. However, extensive document requests or specialized reports may incur processing fees as per the Public Records Act.

Documents are updated regularly. Most reports and policies are current within the last 12 months. Check the publication date on each document for specific timing.
